About

A black belt in martial arts shaped how I approach engineering: show up consistently, master the fundamentals before the shortcuts, and verify a result before trusting it. That discipline is why Origin Navigation shipped with a 38 case test suite before it shipped with a demo video.

I like putting people in the same room around hard problems. I have organized and hosted technical conferences bringing student developers and industry engineers together, judged hackathon teams on the strength of their engineering rather than their polish, and stood in front of faculty at Atlas SkillTech University to teach a session on AI and design thinking, which felt strange the first time and genuinely fun by the end.

Off duty, I am somewhere between an engineer and a wanderer. I write technical articles on LinkedIn when a problem is worth explaining properly, and post travel and everyday life to 12.6K people on Instagram, with the occasional tech event sneaking in. I volunteer with a local tree plantation initiative and a doctors association, and I have learned that the best fix for a problem I have been stuck on for days is usually to go somewhere I have never been and think about something else for a while.
